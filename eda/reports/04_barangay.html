04 · Barangay check: does flood hazard point to where cases are?¶

Goal: the app ranks Iloilo barangays by flood hazard × population. Iloilo has no barangay case data, so we test the idea in Zamboanga City, the only city with usable barangay-level leptospirosis records (2013–2022, see notebook 01).

plt.show()

Takeaway: population alone explains much of the raw count, so hazard has to be judged on the rate, not the count.

Takeaways

  • Barangays with more flood-prone area have somewhat higher case rates: ρ ≈ 0.23, and the 95% CI barely clears zero.
  • The signal comes from the 25-year medium layer. "High" hazard alone shows nothing. The layer is a choice, and it should be stated, not tuned further on 101 barangays.
  • This is weak evidence from one city with sparse counts (60 of 101 barangays ever had a case). In the app, the barangay ranking is presented as estimated exposure, not a prediction of cases, and community flood reports refine it.
  • Recorded cases also depend on where people seek care (detection bias), which hazard maps can't capture.

Summary¶

Mostly men aged 20–49 Target group for prophylaxis and messaging
